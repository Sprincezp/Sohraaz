01 · Born in Kolkata

A vision stitched into cloth

Sohraaz Collections was born in the heart of Kolkata with one simple vision — to make ethnic wear feel stylish, modern, and truly special for today's generation.

We believe traditional fashion is not just clothing. It is identity, culture, emotion — and the memories wrapped into every celebration.

02 · Made by Us, Not by Middlemen

One workshop. Every detail.

We are not just sellers — we are the manufacturer. Every kurta, sherwani and Nehru jacket is designed and stitched at our own Kolkata workshop, checked piece by piece before it reaches you.

That is why the stitching holds, the fabric feels real, and the fit works — and why honest pricing is possible without cutting corners.

03 · The Offline Years

Decades of trust, one showroom at a time

For years, Kolkata walked into our Picnic Garden showroom — for Eid, weddings, puja and first impressions. Every customer was fitted, styled and sent home feeling like the best version of themselves.

The love was so real, so repeated, that one question answered itself: why should only Kolkata have access to this?

04 · Online, The Sohraaz Way

The same warmth — now at your doorstep

We took Sohraaz online so the same quality, style and experience that filled our showroom could reach homes across India.

Same fabrics. Same craftsmanship. Same honest pricing. Just delivered pan-India — with COD, free shipping above ₹999, and the showroom's warmth behind every WhatsApp chat.
